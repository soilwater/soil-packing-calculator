Mass of dry soil per volume. Most packed soils: 1.1–1.5.

Water content given as

Volumetric: cm³ of water per cm³ of soil. Gravimetric: g of water per g of dry soil. Use fractions (20% = 0.20); separate several values with commas.

3Soil and packing

Air-dry soil still holds a little water, usually 0.01–0.04 g/g. Enter 0 if the soil was oven-dried.

Packing layers are not different soils. The same mix is added in a few thin portions, each pressed down to a line. This keeps the bulk density even from top to bottom.

Layers 3–5 cm thick work best.

      How it's calculated
      Dry soilMs = ρb × V
      Gravimetric waterw = θ ÷ ρb
      Soil to weighMs × (1 + w0)
      Water to addMs × (w − w0)

      V = soil volume (cm³), θ = volumetric water content, w0 = water content of the dry soil. Water density is taken as 1 g/cm³.

      Measure the water content of air-dry soil

      1. Weigh an empty can. Do not tare the scale.
      2. Add about 50 g of your air-dry soil and weigh again.
      3. Dry at 105 °C for 48 h and weigh again.
      4. Do 3 cans and enter the masses (g).
